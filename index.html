The approach

This valuation runs on a valuation engine I built from scratch in Python: it reads a company's XBRL-derived financial history, reconstructs a full three-statement forecast, builds a bottom-up WACC from first principles, and produces a discounted cash flow — entirely through live, auditable Excel formulas, never pasted values. The same engine has been run on Suzlon, Adani Ports, and two IT-services comparables (LTIMindtree, Mindtree) without a single line of company-specific code.

Every schedule ties out to the as-filed numbers: revenue, working capital, PP&E, debt and tax all reconcile to zero against the reported financials before a single forecast year is built. The market inputs — share price and shares outstanding — are pinned to the actual close on 26 September 2025, not a live snapshot, so the valuation is a genuine point-in-time exercise, not a moving target.

The honest read

The market-implied rate of 36.9% sits between the fundamental-growth estimate (19.7%) and the sell-side estimate (48.5%) — it isn't an outlier. If anything, a real analyst note published before this valuation date was already more bullish than what the market price required. The gap worth watching is the fundamental-growth check: Suzlon's own ROIC and reinvestment rate support closer to 20% growth, not 37% — the difference is exactly the premium the market was placing on Suzlon's order-book momentum and sector tailwinds holding up.
